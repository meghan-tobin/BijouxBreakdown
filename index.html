how to use bijoux breakdown

1. + Configure Supplies
Create a supply configuration for a type of jewelry (e.g. "Earrings"). Add each material with its name, total cost, quantity, and unit. Hit Save when done.
2. + Add Items
Create a specific item (e.g. "Dinner at Pearls"). Select a configuration, enter how much of each supply you use, and your production cost is calculated automatically.
3. Board
Saved items appear as cards. Drag them around, resize, and group them into collections. Breaking an item out of a group takes a bit of a pull.
All data is saved in your browser.
